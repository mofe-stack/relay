relay veritas ai hackathon 2026

make your sound.
relay says the word.

relay doesn't need words said the standard way. for each word you want, make your own sound for it three times: the word however you can say it, a hum, or any sound you can repeat. then make the sound, and relay says the word clearly, or shows the three closest words when it isn't sure.

this project is a prototype and is not medical advice. everything runs in this browser and no audio leaves the page. the first load downloads whisper small's listening half (about 92 mb).
